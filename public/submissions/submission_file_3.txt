USABILITY TEST SUMMARY

Participants: 4 teaching assistants and 2 instructors
Tasks: locate an assignment, inspect submissions, update a grade, and move to the next student

FINDINGS

- Participants understood the assignment table without instruction.
- The active navigation state needed stronger contrast.
- Reviewers preferred comments attached directly to the relevant passage.
- Persistent grading actions reduced scrolling during batch grading.

NEXT STEPS

Add passage-level comments, improve active-state contrast, and test the grading workflow with longer submissions.
